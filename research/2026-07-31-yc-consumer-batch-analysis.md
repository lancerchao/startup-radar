## Direct reconciliation with ideas discussed and killed this session

**"Personal memory layer across AI apps" → Egoist Machines.** My kill reasoning was structural: the platforms holding the memory (OpenAI, Anthropic, etc.) are strategically required to refuse interop, so an outside layer can't get the access it needs. Egoist Machines is funded anyway — but on pure founder pedigree (an OpenAI ML researcher + AI-security researcher + repeat founder), zero traction, and their "AI Passport" framing reads as a policy/data-portability bet (GDPR-style forced access), not a bet that platforms cooperate voluntarily. That's a path only available to founders with real AI-policy standing — not reachable for a solo founder without that credibility. The structural kill for Lance stands; this shows *why this specific team* got the exception.

**"Professional discovery via voice interview" → Pluto.** This directly validates the reframe I gave: don't build the discovery network, sell the interview/sourcing tool into networks that already have distribution. Pluto's paying customers include **Mercor itself** — they're B2B infrastructure for existing marketplaces, not a competing platform. Exactly the "sell the wedge" advice, now proven funded and paying.

**"TikTok for UGC games" → not just Playabl.ai.** Three more funded entrants in the same 9 months: Instaplay, Pops, and CodeWisp (games-adjacent). That's 4-5 YC-backed teams plus Roblox all chasing the same space concurrently — confirms the "already crowded" kill even harder than known at the time, not weaker. The one with real edge (Playabl.ai) had a prior revenue-generating UGC gaming business before starting; the "unclear" ones (Instaplay, Pops) are the batch's own hedge bets and read as the weakest theses in the entire set.

## Lessons for Lance's own idea pipeline

1. **Traction and pedigree are substitutes, not requirements of the idea itself** — the same "TikTok for X" or "AI Y" pitch gets funded or doesn't purely on whether the team already has the edge in hand. Score the team's edge before the idea.
2. **When YC funds multiple teams in one space in one cycle (games: 4-5; AI-personal-agent: jo, ED1TH, Remix, Egoist Machines all adjacent), that's a hot-pattern signal, not a green light** — it means the space is being actively contested by better-resourced entrants right now.
3. **The "unclear/thesis bet" bucket is small (5/28) and clusters in copycat categories** — YC's own bar for funding on vibes alone, without traction or pedigree, is higher than the 2021-era meme suggests.
4. **Repeat founders in the exact same failed space (Drafted) still get funded** — pattern-matching isn't infallible; worth remembering both directions when reading "this failed before" as a kill signal.
5. **Vendor-claimed numbers in YC's own launch posts are frequently unverifiable and sometimes implausible on their face** (Result's $350K ARR/beta-user average) — treat every "X users, $Y MRR" in a launch post as a claim, not a fact, exactly as the radar's own research discipline already does.
