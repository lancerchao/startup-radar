# AI-Native Senior Dating / Companionship — Thesis Validation Report

*Deep-research run 2026-07-31: 23 sources, 100+ claims extracted, top 25 adversarially verified — 16 confirmed 3-0; the Known-related claims are single-sourced (verification pool hit session limits) and Known's own numbers are vendor-claimed regardless. Sheet row: "AI-Native Senior Dating / Companionship (Tinder for 60+)".*

**Verdict: demand, WTP, and the scam-safety wedge all confirmed. Two de-risk items before anything else: Meta's dating-ads authorization (your channel edge is gated) and the metro liquidity plan.**

## Demand + WTP (all Pew/BGSU, confirmed 3-0)

- 17% of Americans 50+ have ever tried online dating, but only **3% are currently active** (vs 15% of under-50s) — huge latent pool, low active penetration. Read: the market is real but adoption friction (fear, UX) is the thing to solve, which is the product thesis.
- 50–64 ever-use: 20%; 65+: 13%.
- **41% of dating-app users 30+ have paid** (vs 22% under 30) — older users monetize better.
- Gray divorce rate doubled 1990→2010; **~40% of all divorcing Americans are 50+** — structurally growing newly-single supply.
- OurTime (Match Group) charges **$39.99/mo** ($19.99/wk, $89.99/6mo + paid boosts) at a **3.9/5 rating (#163 Social Networking)** — premium pricing on a middling decade-old product.

## Scam/safety wedge (confirmed)

- **47% of online daters 50+ have encountered a suspected scammer** (Pew).
- **Women 50+ report 57% negative experiences vs 38% for men** — and women are the scarce supply side (SilverSingles 59% female overall but engagement quality is the issue). Safety is the female-supply lever, i.e., the wedge and the liquidity strategy are the same feature.
- OurTime's own App Store reviews carry explicit scammer warnings — the incumbent's users are complaining about exactly what this product fixes.

## Distribution — THE catch (confirmed)

**Meta treats dating as a restricted ad category: written permission required before running any dating ads** ([policy](https://transparency.meta.com/policies/ad-standards/restricted-goods-services/dating-ads/)). Application form exists; eligibility criteria are not published, so approval odds for a brand-new app are unknowable from the policy text. **The careerpicai playbook does not transfer automatically.** De-risk step 1: submit the authorization application early (or acquire via the non-dating "companionship/community" framing while pending — needs policy care). Alternate channels: Google UAC, and organic senior-community content.

## Regulatory floor (confirmed)

- NJ Internet Dating Safety Act: safety notifications are **statutory, not a differentiator**; if you don't run criminal background checks you must say so in bold 12-pt caps; if you *do* screen, you must disclose extensive limitations — which **directly constrains "dating with a bodyguard" marketing claims**. Several states have IMBRA-adjacent rules; a 50-state disclosure pass is table stakes before launch.

## Known (the playbook-validator — all single-sourced/vendor-claimed)

$9.7M Series A led by Forerunner (Forbes; TechCrunch adds Pear, Coelius — the "$10M/NFX" framing in press was loose). SF-only beta through 2025 (7,000 beta users), public SF launch Feb 2026, "thousands of users" claimed. Mechanics validate the no-forms thesis: ~26-min voice onboarding sessions, one match at a time, photos withheld until connection, AI books the date, **transactional pricing $15–30/date** (not subscription), claimed 50% match→date conversion and <1% no-show (unaudited). Lesson: the mechanic works well enough to raise on; nobody has pointed it at 50+; transactional per-date pricing is an interesting alternative to subscription for a demo with subscription fatigue.

## Recommended next actions

1. **Submit Meta's dating-ads authorization application now** — it's free, and its outcome decides whether the core distribution edge exists. Kill signal: rejection with no path.
2. Demand probe behind that: landing page + waitlist ("Dating after 60, with a bodyguard"), senior-community organic + Google if Meta pending.
3. Liquidity design before code: single metro, women-first onboarding (safety messaging), the voice-AI interview doubles as content ("real people, real stories") for organic.
4. Kill criteria: Meta authorization denied AND Google CAC >$150; or waitlist <5% conversion; or women's signups <40% of total (supply death); or Known publishes audited traction proving the mechanic but raises a mega-round and announces senior expansion.
