## Ideas

Three candidates deep-dived this cycle, all rejected — the twenty-ninth straight cycle with zero survivors. All three had genuinely fresh, dated triggers (a scandal three months old, a shutdown two days old, a rule not yet even in effect), and all three died to variations on the same finding: the exact mechanic each idea needs is already live somewhere — often owned by the very incumbent the idea is trying to route around.

### 1. Independent cert-history/authentication tool for graded trading cards — REJECTED (new)

- **Brief description:** A lookup tool for trading-card buyers that shows a graded card's full history — has this cert number's grade ever changed, has this exact card image appeared before under a different grade or listing — cross-referencing grading-company population reports and marketplace sold listings, positioned as an independent trust layer following PSA's regrade scandal.
- **Risks:** Every component this idea needs (cert lookup, image-fingerprint fraud matching, cross-marketplace sold-listing data) is already live via 12+ competitors, and the two data assets that would make it defensible — the card image corpus and cross-marketplace sold-listing history — are now literally owned by PSA's own parent company.
- **Consumer pain (quantified):** Real and litigated, but the "15%+ volume shift to Beckett/SGC" figure traces to one uncorroborated article and is actively misleading — PSA's parent, Collectors Holdings, bought SGC (2024) and Beckett (Dec 2025), so all three are now the same company (~80% of US grading volume); the real boycott destination is CGC, the one genuinely independent major grader. PSA's own "$200M in fraud intercepted, +45.3% counterfeit submissions" figure is vendor-self-reported with no independent audit. Two real federal suits are pending (RICO class action seeking $1B/$3B trebled; an antitrust suit over the SGC/Beckett rollups).
- **Why now, not 2 years ago:** Genuine — the scandal is 3 months old and litigation is escalating, but a founder starting today has already missed the Dec 2025-Mar 2026 window when snapshotting grade-change history would have been possible.
- **Why don't existing big players compete:** They already do — PSA's parent runs in-house computer vision on ~240TB of card images pre-grading, and it acquired Card Ladder (cross-marketplace sold-listing data) outright.
- **Why isn't it saturated yet — it is:** At least 12 live products cover pieces of this: GemRate (universal population aggregation, live 6 years), CheckCOA (screens against 25,000+ reported fake/reused certs), CardGrade.io, Digital Grading Company/DeepMint AI (image-fingerprint matching to catch crack-and-resubmit fraud — functionally the exact mechanic proposed), plus eBay's own live image-detection feature and Authenticity Guarantee physical inspection.
- **What will change in the next 2 years:** More consolidation, not less — Collectors Holdings keeps buying the independent pieces of this stack (SGC, Beckett, Card Ladder), narrowing rather than widening any gap a third party could occupy.
- **Competitors:** GemRate, CheckCOA, CardGrade.io, SlabScout, CardIndex, Digital Grading Company/DeepMint AI, Card Ladder (PSA-owned), Card Atlas, CollectorCrypt, eBay's native image-detection + Authenticity Guarantee, plus parallel authentication categories in every adjacent collectible (StockX/sneakers, Entrupy/handbags, Chrono24 Certified/watches, CGC/comics, PCGS-NGC/coins) — confirming this is an over-mined idea shape (B2C_RULES Rule 9), not a fresh one.
- **Who pays / sales motion:** Weakest link — buyers checking before a purchase have low willingness to pay for an infrequent, per-transaction check; sellers wanting a "verified clean" badge compete against free official cert lookups; marketplaces (the most scalable payer) already run their own detection in-house (eBay).
- **Expected CAC / LTV / ARR ceiling:** Low-frequency utility use (a few checks per year per user) fails the recurring-value test on its own; realistic outcome is a feature bolted onto an existing portfolio tracker (Card Ladder, Card Atlas), not a standalone venture — likely a low-single-digit-million ARR ceiling even if executed well.
- **Time to first sellable version / Operational load:** A thin cert-lookup aggregator ships in 2-4 weeks but already exists many times over; the actual differentiator (grade-change history) has a hard cold-start problem — you cannot retroactively produce history for certs you weren't already snapshotting before the scandal, so a realistic build needs months of scraping (against PSA's ToS, which explicitly prohibits data redistribution) before the core feature has any coverage, plus real defamation exposure from false-positive "this slab was swapped" calls.
- **Sources:** [Athlon Sports — PSA grading scandal](https://athlonsports.com/collectibles/psa-grading-scandal-sports-cards-2026), [ValueAddedResource.net — antitrust lawsuit](https://www.valueaddedresource.net/psa-collectors-antitrust-lawsuit/), [Sports Collectors Daily — PSA parent acquires Card Ladder](https://www.sportscollectorsdaily.com/psa-acquires-card-laddere/), [Collectors Tech Blog — in-house computer vision](https://blog.collectors.com/image-search/), [GemRate](https://www.gemrate.com/universal-search), [CheckCOA](https://checkcoa.com/psa-graded-cards-certificate-lookup/), [digitalgrading.ai — DeepMint AI](https://www.digitalgrading.ai/blog/how-ai-detects-fake-altered-cards-digital-grading-company-s-deepmint-ai-fraud-protection), [eBay — Trading Cards Image Detection](https://community.ebay.com/t5/Trading-Cards-Image-Detection/gh-p/Image_Scan), [Entrupy — State of the Fake Report 2026](https://www.entrupy.com/report/state-of-the-fake-report-2026/).
- **Kill reason:** The mechanic already exists in fragments across a dozen live competitors, and the two data assets that would make it defensible are already owned by PSA's own parent company — a low-frequency utility layered on top of one real but already-being-exploited news cycle.
- **B2C_RULES gate check:** 1 of 9 gates PASS (Rule 6, niche audience real). Fails Rule 1 (no verified Meta audience — post-2025 interest-category consolidation makes "sports card collecting" untargetable with confidence), Rule 2 (infrequent per-transaction check, not a renewing habit), Rule 3 (no moat — the defensible data is incumbent-owned), Rule 4 (marginal — the real differentiator needs data infra that's already built better elsewhere), Rule 5 (single-player utility, no referral mechanic), Rule 7 (scraping against ToS + defamation exposure + wading into active RICO/antitrust litigation), Rule 8 (reactive to time-decaying news, not a structural distribution story), Rule 9 (decisively over-mined — the identical "authentication checker" shape already ships in cards, sneakers, handbags, watches, comics, and coins). Rejected outright per the ledger's own rule: fail one gate, it's a pass.

### 2. F/J/I visa Duration-of-Status deadline tracker for international students — REJECTED (new)

- **Brief description:** A deadline-tracking and document-assembly tool responding to DHS's Sept 15, 2026 elimination of open-ended student/exchange-visa status, sold either direct to individual international students, to university international-student-services (DSO) offices, or to immigration attorneys.
- **Risks:** A wrong deadline calculation doesn't just produce a bad document — it causes real accrual of unlawful presence and multi-year reentry bars for the user, the same concentrated-liability shape that has already killed AI will-drafting and ERISA-appeal ideas on this ledger.
- **Buyer pain (quantified):** Real and severe — missing the new Admit Until Date without a timely Form I-539 filing starts the unlawful-presence clock (180+ days triggers a 3-year reentry bar, 1+ year triggers 10 years), with no grace period if USCIS denies the extension. But the rule itself is contested: a coalition sued Aug 18 to enjoin it, with a hearing Sept 9 — six days before the rule's effective date and one week from this digest.
- **Why now, not 2 years ago:** Genuinely fresh trigger, but unusually fragile — a founder building today is building on regulatory sand that could be enjoined or narrowed within days.
- **Why don't existing big players compete:** They already do. Terra Dotta launched "ISSS Next Generation" at NAFSA 2025 (standing-room-only reception) explicitly marketed around lifecycle/legal-standing tracking for 700+ institutions; Docketwise and INSZoom already track generic case deadlines for immigration attorneys, making an Admit Until Date field a trivial roadmap addition, not a wedge.
- **Why isn't it saturated yet — it isn't fully occupied, but the reachable slice is:** The underlying data (a traveler's own Admit Until Date) is a free, instant lookup at CBP's own i94.cbp.dhs.gov — not proprietary. On the consumer side, TrackMyOPT, VisaBuddy, DueVisa, and PriorityPath already ship F-1/OPT/visa deadline tracking with tiered alerts at low price points (~$5/mo).
- **What will change in the next 2 years:** Litigation resolves one way or another; either the rule is vacated (killing the trigger entirely) or it stands and incumbents (Terra Dotta, Docketwise) finish absorbing it as a routine feature.
- **Competitors:** Terra Dotta, Sunapsis, Mobility Online (institutional); TrackMyOPT, VisaBuddy, DueVisa, PriorityPath, F-1 Compass (consumer); Docketwise, INSZoom/Mitratech (attorney case management).
- **Who pays / sales motion (honest):** B2B is a slow institutional RFP/procurement sale (FERPA/security review, 6-18 month cycles) — a bad fit for a solo founder with no enterprise sales experience. B2C is genuinely self-serve and fast, but lands in a small, price-sensitive, already-competitive, and per Open Doors' own 2025/26 data, shrinking population (international enrollment reportedly down ~17% YoY amid the hostile visa climate).
- **Expected CAC / LTV / ARR ceiling:** B2C likely sub-$1-2M ARR ceiling at ~$5/mo against entrenched low-price incumbents in a shrinking TAM; B2B has a real per-account ceiling ($5-20K/year, Terra Dotta's anchor) but requires the multi-year institutional sales motion this founder profile doesn't have.
- **Time to first sellable version / Operational load:** Technically days-to-weeks (an MVP is a reminders wrapper on public I-94 data) — which is exactly the red flag: zero technical moat, and ongoing indefinite regulatory-monitoring burden to keep deadline logic correct through active litigation and likely future policy chaos (echoing the 2025 SEVIS mass-termination episode).
- **Sources:** [Federal Register — final rule](https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/2026/07/17/2026-14439/establishing-a-fixed-time-period-of-admission-and-an-extension-of-stay-procedure-for-nonimmigrant), [Fragomen — lawsuit to enjoin](https://www.fragomen.com/insights/united-states-lawsuit-seeks-to-enjoin-implementation-of-final-rule-eliminating-duration-of-status-admissions-for-f-j-and-i-nonimmigrants.html), [Inside Higher Ed — Terra Dotta ISSS Next Gen](https://www.insidehighered.com/news/student-success/college-experience/2025/06/05/new-ed-tech-tools-international-student-support), [CBP I-94 lookup](https://i94.cbp.dhs.gov/), [FTC — DoNotPay final order](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/02/ftc-finalizes-order-donotpay-prohibits-deceptive-ai-lawyer-claims-imposes-monetary-relief-requires), [IIE — Open Doors 2025](https://www.iie.org/news/open-doors-2025-press-release/), [Poets&Quants — 17% enrollment drop](https://poetsandquants.com/2025/11/17/open-doors-report-17-drop-in-international-student-enrollment-in-u-s-this-fall/).
- **Kill reason:** The underlying data is a free government lookup, incumbents already cover deadline tracking on both the institutional and attorney side, a wrong calculation exposes a solo founder to uninsurable personal liability for causing real reentry bars (the same UPL-adjacent shape that killed will-drafting and ERISA-appeal ideas here), and the rule itself faces an injunction hearing before this idea could even ship.
- **Not scored against B2C_RULES** — liability profile alone is disqualifying regardless of gate scoring; B2B arm is enterprise-sales-shaped and explicitly a poor fit for this founder's stated preference for self-serve/founder-led sales.

### 3. Lightweight reorder-forecasting/PO app for Shopify merchants (post-Stocky) — REJECTED (new)

- **Brief description:** A Shopify App Store app doing inventory reorder-point forecasting and auto-generated purchase orders, priced cheap or one-time, targeting merchants displaced by Shopify's Aug 31, 2026 shutdown of its own Stocky app.
- **Risks:** The "Stocky refugee" niche reads like a gap but is a fully-fought-over sub-market with a 7-month head start against this idea.
- **Buyer pain (quantified):** Real but narrower than framed — Stocky required Shopify POS Pro ($89+/mo/location) or Plus, so displaced users skew toward physical/omnichannel retailers, not the median online-only Shopify store. The shutdown was telegraphed 13+ months out (key features stripped July 2025, delisted from new installs Feb 2026, dead Aug 31) — not a surprise event.
- **Why now, not 2 years ago:** Weak — Cogsy and Prediko, two of the most-cited "Stocky alternatives," raised $6M and $6.65M seed rounds back in 2021-2022, years before this shutdown; the trigger accelerates an already multi-year-old, already-capitalized market rather than opening a new one.
- **Why don't existing big players compete:** Shopify itself deliberately chose not to rebuild Stocky's forecasting/PO features into core Admin (stated rationale: consolidation), leaving the space to third parties — but that space is already filled.
- **Why isn't it saturated yet — it is, decisively:** At least 15-20 apps are actively fighting over this exact query right now, several with real traction: Assisty (306+ reviews, 4.9/5, free tier + $19/mo), Prediko (228 reviews, 4.9/5, markets directly as a Stocky replacement, from $49/mo), EasyScan (329 reviews, 5.0/5, "Built for Shopify" certified), Genie, Monocle, Cogsy, Katana, Ordoro, Stock Sync (1,299 reviews), plus a fresh wave of small entrants (Forstock, IFH, Automat, Sensible) that shipped competing MVPs within months of the Feb 2026 delisting. At least 15 separate companies have published "Stocky is shutting down / alternatives" content since Feb 2026, all targeting the identical search query this idea assumes is open.
- **What will change in the next 2 years:** Nothing favorable — Shopify's ranking algorithm rewards review velocity, install velocity, and "Built for Shopify" certification, all of which entrenched competitors already hold and compound over time; the gap only closes further.
- **Competitors:** Assisty, Prediko, Genie, Monocle, EasyScan, Cogsy, Inventory Planner (Sage-owned), SoStocked, Katana Cloud Inventory, Ordoro, Stock Sync, Forstock, IFH, Automat, Sensible Inventory Forecasting.
- **Who pays / sales motion:** Nominally self-serve (App Store install, in-app billing, no sales call to start), same as every competitor — but inventory/PO data integration is support-heavy in practice, and reviews of incumbents repeatedly cite support responsiveness as a differentiator, meaning real support load even in a "self-serve" model.
- **Expected CAC / LTV / ARR ceiling:** Market is anchored to $19-$300+/mo subscriptions with no one-time-purchase precedent found anywhere in the category; the free-tier/$19-mo low end is already well-reviewed and occupied (Assisty). Against 15-20 ranked competitors with review-based discovery advantages, realistic ARR ceiling for a lone new entrant is low-six-figures at best absent a genuine differentiation angle, and none surfaced in this research — feature sets across competitors are convergent.
- **Time to first sellable version / Operational load:** A basic MVP ships in weeks — which is exactly why 5+ small apps already shipped competing MVPs within months of the Feb 2026 delisting; ongoing operational load is real regardless (inventory/PO bugs cause stockouts and cash-flow harm for merchants, driving support burden).
- **Sources:** [inFlow — Stocky App Sunsetting](https://www.inflowinventory.com/blog/stocky-shopify-app-sunsetting/), [Prediko — Stocky Sunsetting guide](https://www.prediko.io/stocky-sunsetting), [Prediko — 14 Best Stocky Alternatives](https://www.prediko.io/stocky-shopify-app-alternatives), [Assisty pricing](https://www.assisty.ai/pricing.html), [Tracxn — Prediko funding](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/prediko/__glQ591azhtX3IlDT-j7rf-1NjZEZcOLAhJO3dkkwxLA), [Tracxn — Cogsy funding](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/cogsy/__ev5b89qI1VlBL_dt3LsiBIrD1GIBCgCgdDYpwmBEby0), [BigMoves Marketing — Shopify App Store ranking algorithm](https://www.bigmoves.marketing/blog/shopify-app-store-ranking-algorithm-overview-of-shopify-app-store-ranking-factors).
- **Kill reason:** The "Stocky refugee" niche is not a gap — it's already saturated by well-funded incumbents (some capitalized since 2021-22, unrelated to Stocky) plus 15+ new entrants that started marketing "Stocky alternative" content the moment delisting was announced in Feb 2026, seven months before this idea could ship.
- **Not scored against B2C_RULES** — B2B, buyer is a Shopify merchant running a small business, not a personal consumer.

**Bottom line:** Twenty-ninth straight cycle, zero survivors. All three candidates rode genuinely fresh, dated triggers — a three-month-old fraud scandal now in federal litigation, a shutdown two days old, a rule not yet even in effect — and all three still died, each to the same underlying pattern: the mechanic each idea needs already exists, usually because the incumbent has both the motive and the data to build it first (PSA's parent bought the very data assets — Card Ladder, SGC, Beckett — that would have made a third party's trust layer defensible; Terra Dotta and Docketwise already track visa-status deadlines as a routine feature; 15+ Shopify apps raced into the Stocky gap within weeks of its announcement, years after two of them were already VC-funded for the same underlying problem). The more durable finding this cycle is procedural rather than a product idea: the DHS Duration-of-Status rule faces an injunction hearing September 9, six days before its effective date — worth a one-line ledger check next cycle regardless of idea-generation value, since an injunction would remove the "why now" trigger for any future revisit of that space.
